Refund Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

At Bubbl.ooo, we want you to be completely satisfied with our service. This Refund Policy explains our 14-day proportionate (pro-rated) refund system, how refunds are calculated, and how cancellations affect your access.

1. 14-Day Proportionate Refund Window

We offer a 14-day refund window from the date of your subscription purchase. If you cancel within 14 days, you are entitled to a proportionate (fractional) refund — you are only charged for the days you actually used, and the rest is refunded to you.

This window applies to first-time subscriptions and plan upgrades (Starter, Growth, Pro). The window is counted from the original purchase date and does not reset on renewal.

2. How the Proportionate Refund Is Calculated

Your refund is calculated on a per-day basis. We divide your plan's monthly price across a standard 30-day billing period to get a daily rate, charge you only for the days used (the day of cancellation counts as a used day), and refund the remainder.

daily_rate     = plan_price ÷ 30
days_used     = (cancel_day − purchase_day) + 1
amount_used  = daily_rate × days_used
refund       = plan_price − amount_used

Day Counting

  • The day you purchase counts as day 1.
  • The day you cancel is also counted as a used day (you retain access for the full day — see Section 4).
  • Only whole calendar days are counted.

Worked Example

Suppose you buy the Starter plan at ₹499 today, then request a refund 5 minutes later (same day):

  • daily_rate = ₹499 ÷ 30 = ₹16.63
  • days_used = 1 (today counts) → amount_used = ₹16.63 × 1 = ₹16.63
  • refund = ₹499.00 − ₹16.63 = ₹482.37

So we deduct only today's usage (₹16.63) and refund the rest (₹482.37). The exact breakdown is always shown to you on the "Manage Plan" page before you confirm the cancellation.

3. Automatic Calculation at Cancellation

You don't need to email us to get a proportionate refund within the window. When you click "Cancel & Request Refund" on your Manage Plan page:

  • The system instantly computes your refund using the formula above
  • You see the full calculation (plan price, daily rate, days used, refund amount) before confirming
  • On confirmation, the refund is submitted to Paddle and recorded on your account

4. Access Continues Until the End of the Cancellation Day

When you cancel, your plan does not shut off immediately. You keep full access to your current plan until 11:59 PM (23:59) of the day you cancel. After that, your account automatically moves to the Free plan.

  • Your subscription status will show as "Canceled" with the exact access end date and time
  • All paid features remain available until that day ends
  • Your chatbot data and configuration are preserved; only features above the Free plan limits become unavailable afterwards

5. Refunds After the 14-Day Window

If you cancel after the 14-day window, no automatic refund is issued. Your subscription will simply not renew, and you retain access until the end of the current day. In exceptional cases (see Section 7), a discretionary refund may still be granted.

6. Paddle Processes All Refunds

Paddle is our Merchant of Record and handles all payment processing, including refunds. When a refund is issued:

  • Paddle returns the funds to your original payment method (card, UPI, PayPal, etc.)
  • You receive a confirmation email from Paddle
  • The refund appears on your statement from Paddle (not Bubbl.ooo)
  • Processing typically takes 5–10 business days depending on your bank or provider

7. Exceptional Circumstances

Outside the standard 14-day window, we may grant discretionary refunds in cases such as:

  • Extended service outage (more than 48 consecutive hours) that materially impacts your business
  • Billing errors or duplicate charges
  • Advertised features not delivered within a reasonable timeframe

Each case is reviewed individually. Email {{ config.SUPPORT_EMAIL }} with the subject line "Refund Request" and your registered email address.

8. Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds:

  • Cancellations made after the 14-day window (no pro-rated refund)
  • Accounts terminated due to Terms of Service violations
  • Custom integration or development work performed on your behalf
  • Free plan accounts (no payment was made)

9. Downgrades and Plan Changes

If you downgrade to a lower-tier plan, the change takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle, and you retain access to your current tier's features until then. Proportionate refunds apply to full cancellations within the 14-day window, not to downgrades.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated via email to active subscribers.
