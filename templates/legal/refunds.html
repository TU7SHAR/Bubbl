Refund Policy

Last updated: July 2026

We want you to be happy with Bubbl.ooo. If things don't work out, here's how refunds work.

1. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. When you cancel:

  • Your paid features remain active until the end of your current billing period
  • Your account automatically downgrades to the Free plan after the period ends
  • Your chatbots and data are preserved (within Free plan limits)
  • No further charges will be made

2. Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a full refund if:

  • You request a refund within 7 days of your initial subscription purchase or renewal
  • You have not extensively used the paid features during that period
  • Your account is in good standing (no violations of our Terms of Service)

After 7 days, refunds are generally not available, but we may consider exceptional circumstances on a case-by-case basis.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  1. Email support@bubbl.ooo with the subject line "Refund Request"
  2. Include your registered email address and reason for the refund
  3. Our team will review and respond within 2 business days

4. Processing & Timeline

All payments and refunds are processed through Paddle, our merchant of record. Once approved:

  • Paddle initiates the refund to your original payment method
  • Refunds typically appear within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank
  • You will receive a confirmation email from Paddle when the refund is processed

5. Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds:

  • Free plan accounts (no payment made)
  • Subscriptions older than 7 days from purchase/renewal date
  • Accounts terminated due to Terms of Service violations
  • Partial-month usage after cancellation (you retain access until period ends)
  • Add-on message packs or one-time purchases (if applicable)

6. Disputes

If you believe a charge was made in error, please contact us before filing a dispute with your bank. Chargebacks may result in account suspension. We are happy to resolve billing issues directly.

7. Contact

For refund requests or billing questions:

Email: support@bubbl.ooo
Platform: app.bubbl.ooo
Response time: Within 2 business days
